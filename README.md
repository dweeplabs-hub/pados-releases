# Pados — Android releases

Builds of the Pados app for the Andaman & Nicobar Islands. **Binaries only —
there is no source code in this repository.**

### Just want the app?

**→ [padosandaman.in/download](https://padosandaman.in/download)**

That page always points at the newest build here, and it explains the two
things worth knowing before you install: Android will ask your permission
first, and this file is signed with our key rather than Google's.

Or take the APK straight from
[Releases](https://github.com/dweeplabs-hub/pados-releases/releases/latest).

### What Pados is

One app, two sides. Shoppers find the stores near them and order for pickup
or delivery. Shopkeepers get billing, a catalogue, inventory and sales
reports — free, with a monthly order allowance that does not expire.

Pados takes no commission on anything a shop sells, and customers pay no
platform fee.

### Why this repository is public when the app's source is not

An APK is not a secret. Anyone who installs the app already has it, and can
pull it off their own phone in a minute. What a public release needs to be is
*reachable* — a private repository cannot serve a download to someone who has
no GitHub account, which is every merchant we have.

So the binaries live here and the source stays where it is.

### Installing over a Play Store copy, or the other way round

Android will refuse. Google signs the Play Store copy with its own key and
this one is signed with ours, so the phone treats them as two different apps
and says *App not installed*. Remove one before installing the other.

Nothing of yours is lost when you do. Your shop, products, stock, bills,
orders and customers are on our servers, not on the phone — sign in again
with the same account and it is all where you left it.
